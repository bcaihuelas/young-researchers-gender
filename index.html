About the conference

The conference brings together researchers working on gender inequalities across the social sciences at different points of their careers, and gives them a space to present ongoing work, receive prepared comments and build contacts beyond their own institution.

Every paper has assigned discussants who read it in advance, so the emphasis falls on collaboration and constructive feedback rather than on presentation alone.

Four thematic panels cover political behaviour, socialization, backlash and the far right, labour markets, care work, migration, representation and gender-based violence. A closing roundtable brings together senior scholars to discuss the main challenges facing gender research today.

Everyone is welcome to attend any session, and undergraduate and master's students are particularly encouraged to come.
