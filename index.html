About the conference

The conference brings together early-career researchers working on gender inequalities across the social sciences, and gives them a space to present ongoing work, receive prepared comments and build contacts beyond their own institution.

Four thematic panels and a closing roundtable cover political behaviour, socialization, backlash and the far right, labour markets, care work, migration, representation and gender-based violence. Each panel is chaired by a postdoctoral or mid-career researcher and has two discussants, so that every paper is discussed rather than simply presented.

Undergraduate and master's students are welcome to attend any session.
